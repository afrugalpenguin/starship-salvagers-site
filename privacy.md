# Starship Salvagers: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24th November 2026

Starship Salvagers is made by afrugalpenguin, an independent developer based in the United Kingdom ("I", "me"). This policy covers every build of the game: the full game and the free demo on Steam, and the web version on itch.io.

## The short version

- The game works fully without sending anything anywhere.
- If you agree, it sends anonymous statistics about your runs, so I can balance the game. It is off unless you turn it on.
- No account, no name, no email, no IP address, no location, no cookies, no ads, and nothing is sold.

## What the game stores on your computer

Your save (progress, unlocks, settings) stays on your computer. If you turn on run statistics, the game also stores a random install ID and a short queue of runs waiting to be sent. Nothing is stored for statistics until you say yes.

On Steam, all of this lives in the game's folder in your user data folder (inside `%APPDATA%` on Windows, inside `~/.config` on Linux and Steam Deck). Uninstalling the game through Steam does not remove that folder; delete it yourself to remove your save and these files. In the web version, they live in your browser's storage for the game's page, and clearing that site's data removes them.

Turning statistics off deletes the queue of runs not yet sent. RESET ID in Settings replaces the install ID with a new one, so later runs cannot be linked to earlier ones.

## Run statistics (opt in)

After your first run ends, the game asks once whether you want to share anonymous run statistics. Nothing is sent unless you say yes. If you say yes, the run you just played is sent too. If you say no, nothing at all is sent, not even your answer. You can change your answer at any time in Settings.

If you say yes, the game sends:

- a random install ID, made up on your computer and not linked to you, your Steam account or your device
- a random ID for each play session and each run
- the game version and edition (full game or demo)
- what happened in each run: the contractor, mode and tier, Endless level and modifiers, the run's seed, when the run started and how long it lasted, each room and how it went, aliens killed by type, damage taken by source, upgrades offered and taken, scrap, speed bonuses, boss fights and their phases, how the run ended and what ended it, score, best multiplier, dashes, and shots fired and landed
- how smoothly the game ran: average frame rate and slowest frame
- your screen width, rounded to a broad band
- whether you picked one of the game's suggested ship names, and whether you found a hidden ship name easter egg (never the name itself or anything else you type)
- three small events outside runs: that you said yes, when one particular achievement unlocks, and on the demo's end screen, that it was shown and which button you chose

Each run is at most 45 KB. A very long run drops some rooms from the middle to stay under that.

PostHog also keeps a short profile for each install ID, built from the runs above: the edition, the first and latest game version, the first and latest contractor, and the latest mode, tier and result.

It does not send your Steam ID, your name, your IP address, your location, or anything you type. The game sends events directly, without a tracking library: no cookies, no session recording, no automatic tracking of clicks or screens. Each event tells the receiving service not to record an IP address or look up a location, and the service is also set to discard IP addresses.

## Who receives it

Run statistics go to PostHog, an analytics service, on its EU hosting. PostHog processes them on my behalf and for no other purpose. I use them only to balance and improve the game. I do not sell or share them with anyone else.

## How long it is kept

Run statistics, and the profile for each install ID, are kept for up to one year, then deleted. If that ever changes, this policy will say so first.

## Legal basis

I process run statistics only with your consent (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time in Settings.

## Your rights

You can turn statistics off at any time in Settings, which stops all sending and deletes anything not yet sent. Because the statistics are anonymous and the install ID is random and not linked to you, I cannot tell which statistics came from you, so there is nothing that identifies you to access or delete. Statistics already sent are deleted automatically after one year. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

## Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change, and a significant change will be announced on the game's Steam page.
